# Dominion of a Thousand Suns: race traits and storylines review

*Main Claude, 28 Nov 2026. Based on the race data, all 16 sagas and 18 older storylines, a 250-turn Kethrak playtest, and 30 fresh AI-only games (medium map, 6 empires).*

## The short version

- **The races are in decent shape on numbers**, but too many of them are made different by "+20% production" rather than by a rule you can feel. Nine of the 18 races now have production of 1.15 or more. Each time a race was weak we gave it more production, so the races are slowly turning into the same race.
- **The sagas are well written and well built** (4 acts, 3 versions of each act, forks, flags, 4 endings each). They had two real problems, and I fixed both today:
  1. **Nine games in ten got the boring ending.** A special ending needed you to lean hard one way on almost every choice. Across all 16 races, a player who picks at random reached a special ending only 6–18% of the time. I lowered the bar a little: special endings now play about 4 times in 10. The dark "civil war" endings keep the old, hard bar, so you only get one by being truly ruthless.
  2. **The choice rewards were too small to matter.** "+200 research" is big at turn 25 and nothing at turn 200. Rewards now grow with the game: about x2.75 at turn 140 and about x3.5 at turn 200. An empire in the smaller half of the galaxy also gets its gains x1.5 on top, so the story becomes a lifeline for whoever is falling behind (your idea for helping weak races). The event window shows the real, scaled numbers. The two ending rewards that give research went from 500 to 1500 points.
- **One thing on the race screen is fake:** "Travel: warp / phase / lanes / tunnels / gates" does nothing in the game. It is flavour text. We should either make it real or take it off the race screen. My vote: make it real, small (see the ideas at the end).
- **The older 6-chapter storylines are now unused for 16 races**, because each saga replaces its race's storyline. Only **Ruuvak and Tarkhan** still use the old ones, because nobody has written their sagas yet. They are also two of the weaker races, and that is no coincidence: their stories are thinner. → A Gemini job: Ruuvak and Tarkhan sagas.
- **No crossovers have been written.** The engine already supports beats that play when two named races meet ("the Kethrak meet the Verdane"), but there are 0 of them. That is the cheapest way to make the galaxy feel alive. → A Gemini job.
- **The endings are samey across races.** About 10 races use the same four: everyone fears us / civil war / our rival becomes an ally / golden age. Only Orrun, Vossari, Velari, Drovani, Gorrath, Ixil, Verdane, Quorai and Raxxan have one twist. I suggest each race gets **one ending that only it can have**, tied to its unique rule (ideas below).

## The sagas: writing quality

- **Strong:** the premises are imaginative and on-identity. The Kethrak "pale queen" in the root-vaults, the mute worker caste, the warm-blooded captives in the nursery pools, the Seraphine sky palace. The choices really are moral forks, not "+money or +research".
- **Weak:** Gemini's prose leans on the same adjectives (ancient, deep, vast, sacred) and the same sentence shapes. The old storylines were worse: every ending opens with "Having X, the Y stand... Now the Z decide whether...". The sagas mostly avoid that.
- **Naming slip:** the Kethrak's fallback ending is titled "The Thriving Canopy". That is a jungle/plant title on an insect hive (and its internal id says "eternal brood"). Small fix: rename it "The Eternal Brood".
- **Players can't see which way they're leaning until the end.** The Legends screen shows "Ruthless, open (a little)". That's good; a hint on each saga choice ("leans Merciful") would make the forks feel intentional.

## Ideas: one ending only that race can have

These are suggestions for your OK; none are built yet.
- **Kethrak:** "The Endless Swarm": larvae colonies every 10 turns instead of 20.
- **Orrun:** "The Galactic Exchange": you collect a cut of every trade deal in the galaxy.
- **Quorai:** "The Star-Choir Wakes": every star you hold, including empty ones, sends migrants.
- **Ruuvak:** "The Tide of Families": every new colony starts with 3x the people.
- **Terran:** "The Accord of Stars": two empires at war with each other are forced into peace.
- **Velari:** "The Last Gate Opens": your gates connect to every awake gate.

## Ideas: make "Travel" real (small)

- **Warp:** normal.
- **Phase:** +1 speed, -1 range.
- **Lanes:** +2 speed between two of your own colonies.
- **Tunnels:** fleets can't be seen while moving.
- **Gates:** your gates wake up faster.

Each is small enough not to upset balance, and it makes the race screen honest.

## What changed in the game today

- Special saga endings are easier to reach (they play in about 40% of games instead of about 10%). The civil-war endings are unchanged.
- Story and saga choice rewards now grow with the turn, with x1.5 on gains for empires that are behind. The event window shows the real numbers.
- The two research endings give 1500 points instead of 500.
- **The walkout:** while the Grand Coalition's Armada is flying at an empire, the Galactic Council will not crown that empire, for up to 30 turns after the coalition forms. In my playtest the Armada was one turn from Helios when the council handed me the galaxy. Now the Armada gets its shot.
- Four helper Claudes are each playtesting two of the struggling races and testing fixes. I'll check their results with my own runs before anything goes in.
